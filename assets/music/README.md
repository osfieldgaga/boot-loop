# Background music

**Default soundtrack: 8-Bit Battle Loop by Wolfgang_.** Ten locally bundled choices. `/dev` provides track switching, pause/restart, and volume. Track, volume, and enabled preference are saved in this browser and reused on `/`. The player route starts music on its first click or keypress and has a separate Music toggle. Sound mutes music and effects together. Combined JSON export includes the music preferences.

All source pages list CC0, verified October 10, 2026. WAV/OGG sources are converted to 160 kbps MP3; supplied MP3s are retained. Source URLs, duration, and SHA-256 hashes are in `credits.json`. Ambient options are labelled separately. The PM selected 8-Bit Battle Loop as the default on October 10, 2026. Explicitly saved browser track preferences still override the default; the remaining tracks stay available in `/dev`.

Audio balance: default music volume is 8% (previously 18%). Older saved music volumes are reduced proportionally once; subsequent manual volume choices are kept. Jump cue gain is doubled from 0.06 to 0.12.
