# Movement presets

Heavy2 is the product manager’s preferred starting point; movement approval is still pending.

The playground opens with **Heavy2 snappier** for review. Open Movement tuning and use **Heavy2 original** / **Heavy2 snappier** to compare. Each selection resets the player. Restore defaults loads the original Sprint 1 baseline.

The snappier candidate keeps every Heavy2 value except:

| Setting | Heavy2 original | Snappier candidate | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jump hold duration | 0.20 s | 0.15 s | Shorter sustained rise; held jumps reach their peak sooner and are lower. |
| Float near the jump peak | 60 px/s | 20 px/s | Less time under half gravity around the peak. |

Launch speed remains −250 px/s, gravity remains 1800 px/s², and all horizontal settings are unchanged. This changes the jump arc, not the delay between pressing jump and launching. Tap jumps should feel very similar; the biggest difference is on held jumps.

Your two exported presets are preserved unchanged. The candidate is `boot-loop-movement-heavy2-snappy-10.05.26.json`. Use Export preset to save further adjustments.
