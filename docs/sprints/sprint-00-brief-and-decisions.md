## Goal

Turn the approved concept into a buildable MVP and identify contest requirements that could change the plan.

## Dependencies

None. Planning can continue while organizer information is pending.

## Planned work

- Record Boot Loop's approved MVP: two players, one toybox arena, one shared boot, Timed mode, horizontal dash, stomps, gunboots, full wrap, solo demo and rematches.
- Separate conditional additions from the MVP: Stock, additional boots/maps and four-player release validation.
- Collect the mission Build-step instructions and relevant Code of Conduct; clarify desktop-only eligibility and the conflicting deadlines.
- Use October 30 at 11:59 PM Pacific as the conservative entry deadline and October 28 as the submission target.
- Confirm the build workflow, contestant eligibility, hosting approach and intended US region. Render remains a recommendation until chosen.
- Define the Timed tiebreak precisely: kills, fewest accepted shots, shared victory; no kills anywhere means draw.
- Choose a simple first boot configuration for prototyping and record the horizontal dash/stomp rule before combat implementation.
- Prepare the character handoff: original Aseprite PNG sheet plus JSON, replaceable assets and distinct player color ramps.

## How to use this checklist

These are standard Markdown task-list checkboxes: change `[ ]` to `[x]` to mark one. For each review item, mark **Pass** or **Skip**, and leave a comment or skip reason. A failed or untested item stays unchecked with notes about what remains. Do not mark both options.

Testing and final acceptance boxes belong to the product manager; the assistant leaves them unchecked. A skip records your scope decision and does not claim that a test passed. Skipping a contest-related review cannot change the organizer's requirements. Feel approvals must be explicit; skipping their review requires an explicit decision to change that gate.
