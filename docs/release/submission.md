**Description:**

Boot Loop is a chaotic online arena game for 2–4 friends. Stomp rivals or fire downward from your boots, dodge with a powerful dash, and loop through every edge of the screen. Random pickups change everyone's boots at once: Scatter spreads the shots, Turbo speeds up the chase, and Ricochet sends bullets bouncing back upward. Choose among three arenas. Play for the most kills or fight to be the last player standing, then rematch in the same room. Share a room link—no login or installation required.
