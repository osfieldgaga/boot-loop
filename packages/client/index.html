BOOT LOOP / WELCOME

Boots on. Chaos on.

Practice alone, or create a room and invite up to three friends. The host starts with 2–4 players.

  • A/D or arrows: move. Both screen edges wrap, horizontally and vertically.
  • Space/Z: jump on the ground. In the air, press again to shoot downward; holding does not autofire.
  • Double-tap left/right on the keyboard or the on-screen arrows within 220 ms to dash. Recharges automatically 10 seconds after use. Jump within the first 100 ms of a grounded dash for a small extra reach; this ends the dash. No extra air jump. Dash cannot attack and does not protect you.
  • Dash recharges in 10 seconds, even if you land or stomp. Empty boots reload automatically in 3 seconds; partial ammo does not refill. Shoot or stomp for a one-hit kill.
  • Shared boots appear at a random exposed platform after 30–45 seconds and disappear if uncollected for 10 seconds. Touching one switches EVERYONE for 8 seconds: pink Scatter fires three-way shots; gold Turbo gives 25% faster running and acceleration; purple Ricochet bounces bullets upward once off a platform. Only one effect at a time. Reload and dash stay the same. Watch the shared countdown.
  • White outline: protected — cannot attack or be hit.
  • Timed: most kills, then fewer shots; no kills is a draw.
  • Stock: last survivor wins. At timeout: lives, kills, fewer shots. If all remaining players lose their final life together, it is a draw.

Eliminated Stock players watch the remaining fight. Rematch resets lives/scores; settings stay shared.

Bullet hit toleranceTune the extra bounding box ↗

Min 0 · Max 8 world px per side · Step 1 · Default 8

Expands the bullet-hit target on all four sides of each player's 8 × 11 px body. Default 8 gives a 24 × 27 px target. This changes bullet hits only; movement, platforms and stomp boxes stay the same. Solid cover still blocks hits.

Minimum
0: the 8 × 11 px body only. Shots need more precise aim.
Maximum
8: a 24 × 27 px target. Near misses count much more often; hits may feel generous.

Open Movement diagnostics and enable collision boxes to see the gold target outline. Practice previews the box; test hit fairness with a friend. Online, only the host can change this between matches, and it applies equally to both players.
